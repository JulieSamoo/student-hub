About the club

A healthier rhythm for student life

Yoga & Wellness Club gives students a chance to step away from pressure and reconnect with balance, focus and wellbeing. Sessions can include mobility work, breathing practice, gentle yoga flow, relaxation and simple habits that support a calmer routine.

The club is designed to feel welcoming rather than intense, so students can join whether they want stress relief, a more mindful weekly routine or simply a calmer space on campus.

Who it suits

For students who want balance

This club suits students who want to improve wellbeing, feel more grounded and make space for healthier routines alongside study and college life.

It works well for beginners, busy students, creative students and anyone who wants a softer, more restorative kind of club experience built around self-care and consistency.

Wellness culture

Connected to wider beauty and wellbeing pathways

New College Lanarkshire has a strong presence in beauty, hair and wellbeing-related study areas, including introductory hair and beauty programmes, hairdressing, beauty therapy, make-up, nails and salon-based learning. That gives this club a natural connection to a wider college culture built around confidence, presentation, care and personal wellbeing.

Yoga & Wellness Club can complement that environment by offering students a space to focus on inner wellbeing as well as outward confidence. It helps create a college experience where self-care, healthy routine and personal growth all belong together.
