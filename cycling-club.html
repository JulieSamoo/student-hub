About the club

Outdoor activity with a social feel

Cycling Club gives students a practical way to enjoy exercise outside the classroom. Sessions can include local rides, simple route planning and opportunities to build confidence through regular outdoor activity.

The club is designed to feel welcoming rather than intimidating, so students can take part at their own pace and enjoy the social side of being active.

Who it suits

For active and outdoors-minded students

This club suits students who enjoy fresh air, movement and meeting people through shared activity. It works well for students who want a more relaxed alternative to indoor clubs.

It can appeal to both confident riders and those who simply want a more social way to improve fitness, routine and wellbeing.

Club energy

From casual rides to active challenges

While many sessions are social and steady, Cycling Club can also bring a stronger sense of pace, motivation and shared challenge. Group riding helps students build confidence and enjoy the energy of moving together.

For some students, that means staying active more consistently. For others, it means enjoying the excitement of speed, focus and momentum in a supportive group setting.
