About the club

A relaxed place to make art

Art Club gives students time to create outside the structure of formal classes. Sessions can include drawing, painting, colour work, observation, visual experiments and simple collaborative tasks.

The atmosphere is intended to be open and supportive, so students can enjoy the process, build confidence and try new ideas without pressure.

Who it suits

Open to different skill levels

This club suits students who already enjoy art, as well as beginners who want a creative outlet and a calm, practical hobby alongside their studies.

It is especially useful for students who like visual thinking, making things by hand, exploring materials or expressing ideas in a more creative way.

Club atmosphere

What sessions feel like

Sessions are social, practical and informal. Students can work independently, talk through ideas, share materials and develop creative confidence over time.

Some students join to keep up a hobby, while others want a break from academic work and a space that feels more expressive and hands-on.
