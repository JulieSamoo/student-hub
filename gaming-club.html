About the club

Play, connect and compete

Gaming Club gives students a place to relax, socialise and enjoy games as part of student life. Sessions may include multiplayer gaming, team communication, mini competitions and shared discussion around strategy, performance and collaboration.

The club is designed to be welcoming to different experience levels, so students can enjoy gaming casually or become more involved in the competitive side of esports.

Who it suits

For social players and esports-minded students

This club suits students who enjoy games, digital culture and shared problem-solving in a team setting. It works well for both casual players and students who are interested in competitive gaming.

It can especially appeal to students who like fast decision-making, teamwork, streaming culture or the wider creative and organisational side of esports.

Esports connection

Linked to a growing college interest in esports

New College Lanarkshire offers an NQ Introduction to Esports, where students explore the UK gaming industry, tournament organisation, branding, performance and live-stream broadcasting. That makes Gaming Club feel even more relevant for students who want their interest in gaming to connect with real skills and future opportunities.

The college has also highlighted esports activity through events and a summer camp, showing that esports is treated as a serious and developing area of student engagement, not just a hobby. Gaming Club can be a natural first step into that world.
