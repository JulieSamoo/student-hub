About the club

A space for belonging and visibility

LGBTQ+ Club gives students a place to connect with others in an environment built on respect, inclusion and mutual support. The club can include social meet-ups, awareness activity, themed discussions and opportunities to celebrate identity and community.

It is not only a club for events. It is also a place where students can feel seen, listened to and supported while building friendships and confidence as part of college life.

Who it suits

For students seeking community and allyship

This club suits LGBTQ+ students who want connection, visibility and a stronger sense of belonging, as well as students who value inclusion, understanding and respectful community.

It can be especially meaningful for students who want a safe and encouraging space, whether they are looking for friendship, confidence, representation or simply a place to feel comfortable.

College support

Connected to a wider culture of inclusion

New College Lanarkshire states that equality, diversity and inclusion are central to college life and says it aims to create an environment where everyone feels welcomed, appreciated and valued. Its equality reporting also highlights a particular focus on the needs of the LGBT community.

The college has achieved the LGBT+ Youth Scotland Foundations Charter Award, appointed a dedicated LGBT+ Student Officer at Motherwell and developed work around Trans+ inclusion and wider student support. That makes this club feel like part of a broader, visible and meaningful commitment rather than a stand-alone activity.
